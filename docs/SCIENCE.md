# Science budget (canon)

Use these numbers unless a later commit changes them.

## Water

- Known near-surface exchangeable ice: ~20–40 m GEL (polar layered deposits ~17–22 m plus glaciers and ground ice).
- That is ~3–4 million km³. Default slider / volume cap.
- Deuteronilus-class northern ocean ~2e7 km³ (~130 m GEL) is larger than known reachable ice.
- Hidden but mapped-ish: SWIM mid-lat ice table, debris-covered glaciers, possible Medusae Fossae ice ~1.5–2.7 m GEL.
- Locked: hydrated minerals (cook out), disputed deep-crust liquid. Not a v1 tap.
- Lost to space over geologic time: do not return it.

## Air and heat

- Today ~6 mbar. Accessible CO2 ~tens of mbar, not 1 bar.
- Dumping known CO2 does not buy melt-class climate by itself (~10 K class, not ~60 K).
- 2024–26 live path: engineered aerosols / nanoparticles, aerogel blankets, mirrors, PFCs. Models discuss ~30 K if maintained.
- Thin air: melt sublimes unless pressure or a lid holds it.
